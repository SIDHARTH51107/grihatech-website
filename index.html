OUR RANGE

One factory, every opening in your building.

Homes, apartments, schools, hospitals and offices. Tell us the opening size and we will suggest the right system.

Sliding windows & doors

Sashes glide on rollers, so nothing swings into the room or balcony. Available in 2, 2.5 and 3 track, with a mesh track option.

Best for: wide openings, balconies, living rooms

Casement windows & doors

Side-hung shutters that press tight against the frame on closing, giving the best seal against dust, rain and sound. Inward or outward opening.

Best for: bedrooms, road-facing rooms, main doors

Tilt & turn, fixed & ventilators

One handle, two actions: tilt the top inward for safe ventilation, or turn to open fully. Plus fixed picture windows and louvred or top-hung ventilators.

Best for: upper floors, bathrooms, kitchens, stairwells

Mesh, grills & glass options

Finish each unit your way: mosquito mesh shutters, safety grills, and a choice of clear, tinted, reflective, frosted, toughened or double glazing.

Best for: ground floors, mosquito-prone areas, privacy
WHY GRIHATECH
